TRADE WITH CONFIDENCE

Compliance matters.

Requirements vary by trailer, equipment, fuel source and trading location. We’ll help you identify the right specialists and questions for areas such as electrical safety, gas installation, food-grade finishes and local trading permissions.

Important

Coffee Trailers Ireland provides practical guidance, not regulatory certification or legal advice. Buyers remain responsible for confirming all requirements for their intended operation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Good questions,
straight answers.

How long does the process take?+

Timelines depend on the chosen build and shipping conditions. We’ll provide an estimated programme with your quotation and update you throughout.

How is my quotation prepared?+

We quote each project individually based on the trailer model, build specification, equipment, finish, shipping, import costs and Irish delivery.

Can you install my coffee equipment?+

We can plan the trailer around your equipment and coordinate suitable local installers. Equipment can be customer-supplied or included by agreement.

Will the trailer be certified for Ireland?+

There is no single certificate covering every mobile coffee business. The checks required depend on the trailer and equipment. We can coordinate relevant specialists, while you confirm food-business and local trading requirements.

Can I customise the colours and branding?+

Yes. Colour, counter finishes, layout and branding can be discussed during specification, subject to manufacturing feasibility and final cost.
